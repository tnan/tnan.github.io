How to play

Player 1 plays capitals A B C D. Player 2 plays small letters a b c d. Pick one of your letters, then tap an empty slot to place it.

The board

A 4×4 grid of small groups — columns A–D, rows 1–4 (A1, B1 … D4). Each small group holds 4 slots.

One of each letter per group

A small group can hold each letter only once, and a capital blocks its small twin. If a group has A, nobody can add A or a there. Use this to block: drop your letter where your opponent needs one.

Two rule sets

Standard  Win with a same-letter line, a small group, or a big group.

Wild  Everything in Standard, plus mixed-letter lines and same-letter big groups — many more ways to win.

Ways to win

Each example shows Player 1 winning. The same shapes work for Player 2 with small letters.

1Same-letter line Standard & Wild

The same letter sitting in all 4 small groups of a column, row, or diagonal (any slot inside each group).

Column — an A in A1, A2, A3, A4.

Row — a B in A2, B2, C2, D2.

Diagonal — a C in A1, B2, C3, D4.

2Mixed-letter line Wild only

Four different letters, one in each small group along a column, row, or diagonal.

A B C D — one each down column A.

3Small group Standard & Wild

All four of your letters inside a single small group (always a mix — same letters can't repeat in a group).

A B C D all inside group B2.

4Big group — mixed Standard & Wild

A big group is a 2×2 block of four small groups. Place four different letters with one in each of the block's small groups.

A B C D — one per group across block A1·B1·A2·B2.

5Big group — same letter Wild only

The same letter placed once in each of a block's four small groups.

An A in each group of block A1·B1·A2·B2.

Assist levels

Beginner — last-move flash, dims groups you can't use, and a guide flash that lights up every column, row, diagonal and big group your move belongs to (the timer pauses while it plays).

Basic — last-move flash + dimmed blocked groups.

Normal — last-move flash only.

Advanced — no aids; you only get a note if you tap a group that already has that letter.

The AI has four levels — Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert. Use the Who plays first switch under the buttons to choose who opens, then press Start Game. Expert plays to win and is designed to be unbeatable when the AI moves first (its default) — treat it as a puzzle; pick Human first if you want a fighting chance. With a timer, each player's clock ticks only on their turn. Mode, rules, AI level, who-plays-first and timer all lock once you press Start Game.
